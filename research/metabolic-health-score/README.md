# Composite Metabolic Score | Metabolic.Health

During my research internship at Metabolic.Health, I co-authored this abstract with the research team on a nine-domain composite score for summarising metabolic health in a UAE metabolic-clinic cohort.

The work developed a clinically interpretable score that can still be calculated when some biomarker results are missing. It was then evaluated for its relationship with incident type 2 diabetes. The score tracked diabetes risk, but baseline HbA1c alone performed better for that specific, glycaemically defined outcome. This is an important result: it helps identify where a broader metabolic-health score may need further validation rather than overstating its value.
