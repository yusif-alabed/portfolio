# Exploratory ESRD Clinical-Data Analysis

I carried out this project to explore whether routine clinical and metabolomic measurements could reveal patterns between people with end-stage renal disease (ESRD) and healthy controls. I used principal component analysis (PCA) and Random Forest classification to investigate the structure of the data.

The dataset came from a public, de-identified study in [MetaboLights](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/metabolights/MTBLS700). I have not copied the source data into this repository; anyone who wants to reproduce the work should obtain it from the original record and follow its terms.

This was exploratory, cross-sectional research. It is not a validated diagnostic model, a clinical prediction tool, or medical advice.
