# LastBite | InnovAIte Hackathon

LastBite was my team's entry for the InnovAIte Hackathon. We built a concept for reducing food waste by making food-expiry information more visible and turning the ingredients people already have into useful recipe suggestions.

Our presentation explains the problem, the proposed product, and how we planned to make food-waste reduction more practical for everyday users. My team reached the finalist stage of the competition.
